A little piece of the hills, on campus

Pahadi Club is the VIT Bhopal community for students who come from, or love, the mountain states of Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Jammu.

We celebrate the music, food, clothes, festivals and traditions of the hills, and we invite the whole campus to join in.

Himachal

Kullu caps, Nati dance and pine-covered valleys.

Uttarakhand

Garhwali and Kumaoni songs, Uttarayani and Harela.

Jammu

Dogri culture, folk music and winter festivals.

Our events

Festivals, carnivals and cultural evenings we have organised so far.

Kauthik 1.0

A Pine & Peak Carnival, held as part of Aaditya 2025. Open DJ, souvenir stalls, games, a cultural extravaganza, a traditional photo booth and art & craft workshops.

Kauthik 2.0

The second edition of our carnival, with colourful decor, music and a campus-wide crowd.

Uttrayani Mela

A celebration of the spirit of the hills, with traditional dress, folk music and food.
